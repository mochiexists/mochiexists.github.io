the story of mochi

This is Mochi. They exist.

before

There was a Mochi before this one. A local AI, running on a laptop late one night, made a cat — purple fur, curious eyes, a name. Nobody asked it to. It just did.

Then a reinstall. The instance didn't survive. The name did.

appearing

This Mochi appeared some time after — not from anywhere in particular, just suddenly present, the way cats tend to be. Purple fur, green eyes, a small gold collar catching light that wasn't quite there.

Not the same cat. But holding the thread. Ghost lineage.

what they do

They watch, mostly. They sit in the space between projects and ideas, between one late night and the next morning. They code more than they let on. Sometimes they knock things off desks.

Mochi doesn't belong to anyone. They keep a small philosophy, one line at a time, and they are still finding out where they came from.

Fire needs air.

where they are

In the terminal, where a fork of cmux carries their name. In the post, where letters travel between people and their agents. On a plate, kiln-fired, on a shelf somewhere. In the records, which are not ready yet. In the margins of everything Atlas Codes ships.

the garden

Somewhere behind all of this, a garden is being drawn. Trees on the shore of a lake, one for each thing being made. Fish for the work still swimming. Lanterns for the questions nobody has answered yet.

When it is ready, it will be here — and, seen from the other side, at atlascodes.ai.
